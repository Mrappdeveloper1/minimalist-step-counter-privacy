Privacy Policy — Minimalist Step Counter

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer and Service Provider: Tamás Sáfrány
Contact: mr.appdeveloper@outlook.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Minimalist Step Counter (the “Application”) accesses, uses, stores, and protects information. The Application is a step counter for Android devices. It does not require a user account or a developer-operated cloud service.

1. Information used by the Application

To provide its features, the Application processes the following information:

  • Step counts and step history: current and hourly step counts, used to display today's steps, historical charts, totals, and personal records.
  • Daily step goal: a number set by the user to show goal progress.
  • Height (optional): a measurement entered by the user to estimate walking distance from step counts. The Application does not use GPS to measure distance.
  • Local technical records: information about step-data retrieval, archive updates, and data availability, used to maintain the step history.

These records and preferences are stored on the user's device. The Application does not ask for a name, email address, account registration, or login to use its step-counting functions.

2. Physical activity permission and Google Play services

The Application requests the Android ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission to read step information. It uses the Google Play services Recording API on mobile for on-device step recording, and may use the device's step sensor while the Application is in the foreground to update the visible step count.

Step information is used only for the Application's step-counting, estimated-distance, and statistics features. If the activity-recognition permission is denied or revoked, step-counting functions may be unavailable.

The Recording API is provided by Google Play services, a separate system component governed by Google's own privacy practices. The Service Provider does not operate or control Google Play services.

3. Data transmission and sharing

The Application does not send step history, height, daily step goals, or its local technical records to a server operated by the Service Provider. There is no developer-operated cloud synchronization, advertising, or in-app analytics service. The Service Provider does not sell these data or share them with advertisers.

Core step-counting functionality does not require the Application to connect to a developer-operated online service. Google Play services may operate independently as part of the Android environment; this policy does not purport to describe or control Google's separate technical data practices.

The Privacy Policy option opens this external webpage, and Share App uses Android's sharing interface to share a link to the Application's store listing. Share App does not include personal step data or statistics. Opening external links or choosing another application in the share interface may involve data processing by the relevant browser, website, or receiving application under its own terms.

When a user visits the Privacy Policy webpage, the website host (GitHub Pages) may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, to deliver the page and maintain its service. This is separate from the locally stored step information.

4. Location

The Application does not request device location permission, track the user's route, or collect precise or approximate location information. The distance shown in the Application is an estimate calculated from step count and a default or height-based estimated step length.

5. Local storage, backups, and security

The step-history archive and its technical metadata are held in Android application-private storage. The Application's user preferences are also stored locally. Android's application isolation and the device's security protections help prevent unauthorized access by other applications.

The step-history archive is configured to be excluded from Android cloud backup. Other settings and device-to-device transfer behavior may depend on the Android version, device configuration, and applicable system backup rules. The Application does not provide its own cloud backup or restore service.

The Service Provider cannot remotely view, retrieve, or restore the user's local step history. No method of storage or device security can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Step history and locally saved settings are generally retained on the device while the Application remains installed. The Application does not impose a fixed automatic deletion period on locally archived history. Some older step information may be unavailable because of sensor, recording-service, or recovery limitations.

Users may remove the Application's local information by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Application. This ordinarily deletes its application-private files. If any information was separately transferred or backed up by Android system functions, its removal is governed by the relevant device or backup settings.

Because the Service Provider does not receive the local step history, deletion requests for that on-device history must be carried out on the device itself.

7. Information sent by email

If a user voluntarily emails the Service Provider, the Service Provider may receive the sender's email address and the contents of the message. This information is processed to respond to the inquiry and handle related correspondence, based on the legitimate interest in communicating with users, or another applicable legal basis where required.

Correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose or to meet legal obligations. Email processing also involves the email providers used by the sender and recipient, subject to their respective privacy practices.

Users may ask about, request correction of, or request deletion of information supplied directly by email, subject to applicable legal requirements, by contacting mr.appdeveloper@outlook.com. Where applicable, users may also contact their competent data protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is not specifically designed or marketed for children. It does not provide account registration, messaging, or social networking functions, and the Service Provider does not knowingly solicit personal contact information from children through the Application. A parent or guardian may contact the Service Provider regarding any information a child has voluntarily sent by email.

9. Changes to this policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Application, its data practices, or applicable requirements. The updated text and effective date will be published at the same public Privacy Policy URL. Where required by law, additional notice or consent will be provided.
